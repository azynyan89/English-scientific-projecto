Trilingual Morphosyntactic & Semantic Adaptation Lab

When an English gaming term enters Kazakhstan's bilingual environment, it passes through two contrasting grammatical systems simultaneously.

1. Grammatical Contrast: Russian Affixation vs. Kazakh Helper Verbs & Agglutination

Russian integrates English roots via prefixes and verb suffixes (-и-ть, за-, на-), whereas Kazakh preserves the borrowed stem as a nominal base and pairs it with light/helper verbs (жасау, салу, болу, алу, шығу) or attaches agglutinative case and personal suffixes.

2. Four Pathways of Semantic Change Beyond Games

How game-specific vocabulary acquires everyday figurative meanings in school and peer conversations.

Narrowing Specialization

Example: feed (фидить) — In standard English, "to give food." In multiplayer games, narrowed strictly to dying repeatedly and feeding experience/gold to the opposing team.

Broadening Generalization

Example: noob (нуб / нубсың) — Shortened from "newbie" (new player), broadened to describe anyone clumsy or inexperienced in sports, math, or daily tasks (68.1% non-gaming use).

Pejoration Evaluative Shift

Example: toxic (токсик) — Shifted from "poisonous substance" to a strong social label for aggressive, insulting, or negative people online and offline (72.2% non-gaming use).

Metaphorical Extension Figurative Transfer

Examples: lag & carry — Saying "my brain is lagging" (63.9% outside games) when tired in class, or "carrying" (затащить, 61.1%) a whole group school project alone.

Next-Generation Project Innovations

Scalable extensions transforming this study from a static glossary into a long-term educational and linguistic resource.

1. Multi-Region Recorded Teen Speech Corpus

Expand beyond single-school convenience sampling by building an anonymized, ethics-approved audio and chat corpus across multiple regions of Kazakhstan to measure exact Kazakh vs. Russian adaptation frequencies.

2. Trilingual Morphological Parser Prototype

Develop a rule-based NLP lookup tool that automatically splits hybrid teen words (e.g., задонатил, стримде, фарм жасау) into English root + Russian/Kazakh grammatical morphemes for language learners.

3. Longitudinal Slang Lifecycle Tracker

Conduct annual cohort re-testing to track which gaming loanwords remain stable in general youth speech (like lag or toxic) and which fade within a single gaming season.
